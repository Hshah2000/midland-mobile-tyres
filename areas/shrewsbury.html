FAQ

Shrewsbury Questions

Do you come out to villages around Shrewsbury?

Yes. We cover the town and the surrounding villages. Call us with your postcode and we'll confirm.

Can you fix a puncture instead of replacing the tyre?

Often, yes. If the damage is in the repairable area of the tread we'll repair it. If it isn't safe to repair, we'll tell you and fit a new tyre.

Are you open 24 hours?

Yes. Midland Mobile Tyres is available 24/7, including evenings, weekends and bank holidays. Call 07376 023691.

What information do you need when I call?

Your location, the make and model of your car, and your tyre size. The size is printed on the side of the tyre and looks like 205/55 R16.
